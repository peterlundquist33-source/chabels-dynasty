POWER RANKINGS

One board. One question: how good is this team right now?

How It Works

This is a win-now board, not a dynasty-asset board. Every team is scored on how much it can win with this season: the quality of the best lineup it can start, plus the depth behind it to survive injuries. Draft picks are not scored.

Roster strengthBest lineup + depth

We fill every starting slot (PG/SG/G/SF/PF/F/C plus three UTIL) with the team's best option, using Hashtag's current-season points ranks. That lineup is about three-quarters of the roster score; the next five players off the bench are the other quarter.

Real resultsAll-play, record, scoring, form

Once games are played, each team also gets a results score from all-play win %, actual record, points-per-game and last-three-week form.

The blendGrows with games played

Preseason the board is pure roster projection. Results weight ramps up each week and caps around 72% by roughly week 12 — so by mid-season the standings, not the paper roster, drive the board.

Draft picksNot included

This board only measures win-now team strength. Pick capital is a dynasty-asset question and is left out entirely.
