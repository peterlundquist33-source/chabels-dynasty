How It Works

This is a win-now board, not a dynasty-asset board. Each team's number blends three things: the best lineup it can start this season, the manager's track record, and — once games are played — this season's actual results. Draft picks are not scored. Open any team to see the players behind its number.

RosterBest 16, Lock-In value

Chabels is a Lock-In league — each player counts one game a week, your pick. So a player is worth his lockable game (a high-percentile night), not his average: we take Hashtag's 2026-27 projection in our scoring, estimate his game-to-game swing, and add the edge you get from waiting for a good one. A team is worth its best 16 of those — the starting 10, then a declining tail.

Track recordThe preseason anchor

Recency-weighted regular-season win % over the league's history (last year counts most), plus credit for playoff trips, finals and titles, and a nudge for teams trending up. A team that finishes top-four every year does not open the board at #7 because its roster looks thin on paper.

This seasonAll-play, record, scoring, form

Once games are played: all-play win %, actual record, points-per-game and last-three-week form.

The blendShifts with games played

Preseason is 50% roster / 50% track record. As games come in, this season's results ramp up (cap ~65% by playoffs) and the track-record anchor fades toward nothing.
